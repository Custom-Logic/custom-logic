The company behind the product

We make the complicated feel workable.

Custom Logic is an independent South African software company. We design, build and operate digital products for organisations whose work does not fit neatly into an off-the-shelf tool.

Our own product, Tenders SA, is a living example of that approach: a procurement intelligence platform that brings discovery, analysis and action into one focused experience.

Our north star: useful software is not the software with the most features. It is the software that helps people make better decisions and get important work done.
