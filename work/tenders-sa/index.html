The problem

Opportunity information is difficult to turn into a decision.

Suppliers face fragmented procurement sources, dense documents and a difficult path from seeing an opportunity to knowing whether it is relevant, achievable and worth preparing for.

The work is connected: finding a tender is only the beginning. Teams also need to understand the requirements, assess readiness, track developments and coordinate a quality response.

The product response

One workflow, from discovery to preparation.

01 / DISCOVER

Find relevant opportunities

Browse and narrow procurement opportunities by category, province, organisation and company fit.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Interpret requirements

Use structured tender information and AI-assisted analysis to understand the work and evaluation criteria.

03 / ASSESS

Evaluate readiness

Connect company information, procurement intelligence and supplier readiness to a practical decision.

04 / PREPARE

Move toward a response

Track developments and use an application workspace to organise preparation work.

What it supports
