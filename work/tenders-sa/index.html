The product

From discovery to prepared response.

Tenders-SA helps suppliers discover OCPO procurement opportunities, understand requirements, assess company readiness, track procurement intelligence and prepare applications.

Its connected capabilities include search and filtering, Tender Radar, document intelligence, Company Intelligence and award data, Supplier Readiness, Procurement Watch, Application Workspace, subscriptions and supported AI-assistant integrations.
