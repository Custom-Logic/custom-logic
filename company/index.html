The company

Built around the work software needs to support.

Custom Logic is a South African product engineering company that builds and operates focused software products, while developing selected systems for organisations with complex workflows.

Why we exist

Useful systems are designed close to real operations.

We founded Custom Logic around a practical observation: important work is often shaped by fragmented information, manual coordination and software that does not quite fit. The answer is not more features for their own sake. It is a clearer system for the decisions and work that matter.

Our owned product, Tenders-SA, keeps us accountable to the full product lifecycle—from understanding a difficult market, through building and launch, to operating and improving a live platform for real customers.

We bring that same product discipline to selected client engagements where a workflow, data challenge or new platform warrants purposeful engineering.

How we operate

Principles for thoughtful product work.

01 / START WITH WORK

Understand before building

We learn the people, decisions, information and exceptions a system must support.

02 / SHIP USEFUL THINGS

Make progress visible

03 / OWN THE OUTCOME

Operate with care

Live products require attention to maintainability, recovery and continuous improvement.
